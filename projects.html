Projects

  • R2SDF FFT pipeline. A radix-2 single-path delay feedback FFT in SystemVerilog on an iCE40 UP5K, for an 8-bit audio equalizer.

  • Tree-planting crew tools. A shovel-mounted tree logger, crew tracking software and an offline LoRa radio mesh, based on my own planting experience.
