0x00 About

I'm a second-year Electrical Engineering student at McGill University, minoring in Computer Science, working toward FPGA engineering roles with a long-term target of low-latency trading hardware at firms like Jane Street, Citadel Securities, and Jump Trading.

Most of my time goes into building things that are correct, not just things that work — which means writing a transactional C++/Verilator testbench before I trust a datapath, and treating a scoreboard mismatch as more interesting than a passing simulation. I write RTL by hand, deliberately without AI coding assistants, because the intuition for pipeline timing and bit-level arithmetic has to be earned.

Outside of FPGA work, I co-lead the radio sub-team on the McGill Rocket Team's avionics group, building dual-band LoRa telemetry for our launch vehicles.
